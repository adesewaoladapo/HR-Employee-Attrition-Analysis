# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Executive Summary
This project evaluates structural attrition drivers across 1,470 employee records to identify key risk factors contributing to staff turnover and inform targeted retention strategies.

## Key Insights
- **Departmental Risk:** Sales exhibits the highest turnover rate at **20.63%**, followed closely by Human Resources (**19.05%**).
- **Compensation Deficit:** Departing employees earn an average of **$4,787/month** compared to **$6,833/month** for retained staff—a $2,045/month (29.9%) compensation gap.
- **Critical Tenure Window:** **58.3%** of all resignations occur within the first two years of employment (Year 0–1 attrition rate averages **34.88%**).
- **Overtime Catalyst:** Staff working overtime face a **30.53%** turnover rate compared to **10.44%** for non-overtime staff (2.92x higher risk).
